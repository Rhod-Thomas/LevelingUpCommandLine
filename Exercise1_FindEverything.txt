use find in all the ways that are useful.

Find follows this general pattern:
find [where][what][action]

different 'where' is fairly easy. 
different 'what' has more options.
and different 'actions' generally have the more complex syntax.

so for [what]
-type		
-name		
-iname
-user		
-maxdepth	
-mmin
-tmin
-perm
-empty
-size
-newer
-regex
#group logic with parenthases.

and for [action]
-printf
-print0
-exec
-ls


for each practice, build and validate the command one argument at a time. 

Practice 1;  
find . -maxdepth 4 -type f -name *.pp -size -10 -amin -20 -user u0_a564 -printf "%s\t%p\n"

Practice 2;
find . -empty

Practice 3
find . -newer filename

Practice 4
find . -regex 

find . -regextype posix-extended -regex ".*\.(jpg|jpeg|png)"
find . -regextype posix-extended -regex ".*data_[0-9]{2}\.txt"
find . -regextype posix-extended -iregex ".*readme\..*"
find . -regextype posix-extended -regex ".*/.{4}\.sh"
